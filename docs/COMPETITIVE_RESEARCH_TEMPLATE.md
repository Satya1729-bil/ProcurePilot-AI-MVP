## Questions
1. What user job does the product help complete?
2. What public evidence supports the capability claim?
3. What is the workflow before and after AI assistance?
4. How are sources, approvals and audit history exposed?
5. What user pain point remains unclear and needs discovery?

## Interview-ready conclusion
"Based on [source/date], product X supports [verified capability]. I hypothesize [pain] may remain for [persona]. I would validate this with [interviews/data], rather than assume it is a confirmed market gap."
